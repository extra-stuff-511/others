# Defining Youth - 
- period between childhood and adulthood
- marked by significant growth, development, and exploration.
- Start to grasp abstract ideas more readily
- individuals begin to develop their identity
- establish social connections,
- prepare for adult roles and responsibilities
- the age range may vary across culture but it is generally cosidered from adolescence (13 to 19) to young adulthood (mid 20s).
- Youth is the most energetic and productive section of a society
- A country's ability and potential for growth is determined by the size and strength of its youth population
- Youth can be a positive force for development when provided with the knowledge and opportunities they need to thrive.
- The Key task of the youth according to Erikson is the achievement of identity where the youth undergoes a crisis im questioning everything and then finding their own meaning and preferences in life.

# Gender -
- Gender Identity - Gender identity refers to how a person chooses to present self both overtly (socially) and covertly (to themselves) based on their own preferences of living and existin.

- Gender Expression - Part of the social identity of a person refering to the aspects about how a person overtly presents themselves in thought emotion and behaviour.

- distinct from biological sex which is determined by physical attributes such as chromosomes, hormones, and reproductive organs, assigned at birth.

- Gender Norms - these are the ways of living accepted and expected by the society. Different societies have different expectations in terms of gender but mostly traditionally it has been a binary since the industrial revolution. These dictate how a person should present themselves socially and how an individual is supposed to live in their day to day life, assigning and diving all the things that a person of a particular gender can or cannot do. traditionally deviation from this binary is considered to be abnormal and is highly discouraged. Deviant people are socially isolated and underpriveledged in every sense.  

- Gender Roles - These are the assigned behaviours, responsibilities that are dicatated by gender norms.
